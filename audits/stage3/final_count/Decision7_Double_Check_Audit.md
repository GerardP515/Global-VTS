## Consistency rulings applied

- **Monitoring from a VTS centre** (Sweden Traffic, as Åland Sea Traffic VTS-0043): linked as VTS coverage under decision 1, counted as supplemental.
- **Named monitoring by a body that is not a VTS centre** (Irish Coast Guard at Tuskar and Fastnet; Panama Canal signal stations): supplemental, not VTS coverage (owner ruling recorded in the decisions file).
- **Spanish Salvamento Marítimo centres:** the Canary Islands centres are included on the same basis as CCS Almería (VTS-0202): the SASEMAR Plan Anual 2025 assigns them traffic supervision of the Canary TSS.
- **Rotterdam "38 nm seawards":** one radial reading applied to TSS-0051 and TSS-0052.
- **Three-year rule (decision 4):** where it was the only reason for exclusion and an official body still shows the service in operation, the link is included by default (TSS-0115/0116, TSS-0153).
- **Boundary touches within source or datum precision** (TSS-0056, TSS-0057, TSS-0089): included by default with coverage "Boundary only". The Liverpool VTS count rests on this rule alone.
