## New positive

| TSS | Result | Coverage | Service | Count |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| TSS-0127 Near the deep-water route leading to Jazan Economic City Port | **New service** | Monitoring | **VTS-0230 Jazan deep-water approach traffic service** | **+1** |

Saudi Aramco's 2025 port rules state that the main arrival/departure channel is an IMO-developed 90 NM approach channel. Inbound vessels using it must call Jazan Commercial Port Control Center on VHF 16 for instructions to proceed, with JPDI Port Control on VHF 09, and maintain both watches during channel passage.

This meets project Decision 5: an official port traffic-control service has an explicit operational responsibility over the approach route associated with the TSS. The record does **not** claim a formally designated SOLAS V/12 VTS area.

Jazan Commercial Port Control and JPDI Port Control are counted as one coordinated approach-service identity, not two.
