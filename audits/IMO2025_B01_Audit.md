## Identity

All five headings match IMO *Ships’ Routeing* 2025 Part B Section I and the project inventory. Historical adoption is IMCO A.284(VIII) 1973, with later amendments (COLREG.2 circulars for Gogland / Sommers geometry; COLREG.2/Circ.38 deleted the Kalbådagrund and Porkkala inshore traffic zones).

Current status: adopted and in force. Fintraffic notes that Gulf of Finland TSS, or parts of them, may be withdrawn temporarily in difficult ice. That is operational suspension, not deletion of the scheme.

Keep TSS-0002 and TSS-0003 separate. IMO Part E records recommended tracks between the Rodsher and Gogland schemes.

## Mandatory reporting — VRS-0001 GOFREP

**Scheme:** In the Gulf of Finland [GOFREP]  
**Project ID:** VRS-0001  
**IMO:** Part I, I-I/1; MSC.139(76) and MSC.231(82); SOLAS V/11  
**States:** Finland, Estonia, Russian Federation  
**Centres:** Helsinki Traffic (north of the Central Reporting Line); Tallinn Traffic (south of the line, west of 026°30'E); St. Petersburg Traffic (east of 026°30'E, south of the line)

GOFREP is one reporting scheme. It is not three VTS records.

### Named in the official GOFREP geometry

Fintraffic Master’s Guide and MSC.231(82): the western part of the Central Reporting Line is drawn through the midpoints of the separation zones of the TSS **off Kõpu, Hankoniemi Peninsula, Porkkala and Kalbådagrund**.

That is direct authority evidence for:

- TSS-0004 **within_reporting_scheme** VRS-0001 — confirmed
- TSS-0005 **within_reporting_scheme** VRS-0001 — confirmed

Kõpu and Hankoniemi are B02 / later records. Do not attach them to B01.

### Not named

The same Fintraffic pages list **four** Gulf of Finland TSS (Hankoniemi, Porkkala, Kalbådagrund, Kõpu) and then say Russian territorial waters have TSS maintained by Russia. The Master’s Guide text retrieved on 29 September 2026 does **not** name Sommers, Hogland/Gogland or Rodsher.

TSS-0001, TSS-0002 and TSS-0003 sit in the eastern gulf, near or in Russian waters, east of 026°30'E. St. Petersburg Traffic monitors that GOFREP sector. Geographic neighbourhood is not enough. Until the GOFREP IMO annex or a Russian authority page names those three schemes, the reporting link stays **unresolved**.

Applicability (GOFREP): ships of 300 GT and over; specified smaller ships if NUC, at anchor in a TSS, RAM, or with defective navigational aids.

## VTS

### Finnish territorial VTS — do not conflate with GOFREP

Fintraffic operates Hanko VTS, Helsinki VTS and Kotka VTS in Finnish territorial waters. Helsinki Traffic (GOFREP) works from the same Gulf of Finland VTS Centre. That shared building does not make GOFREP a VTS, and it does not make Helsinki VTS cover the offshore IMO TSS.

- Helsinki VTS Sector 2 includes a western (Porkkala) sector for coastal fairways. No opened Master’s Guide page states that the VTS area includes TSS-0005.
- Helsinki VTS Sector 2 also uses Kalbådagrund as a **fairway reporting point** in older NGA text. A fairway call point is not coverage of TSS-0004.

VTS association for TSS-0004 and TSS-0005: **not established**.

### Russian regional VTS — lead only

Rosmorport describes a regional VTS (RSUDS) for the eastern Gulf of Finland, with radar posts on Seskar, Sommers and Gogland, and port VTS at St Petersburg, Vysotsk, Primorsk and Ust-Luga. That is credible infrastructure next to TSS-0001 and TSS-0002. It is not a users’ guide that names those IMO schemes as inside a defined VTS area.

No VTS-0xx issued from this batch.
