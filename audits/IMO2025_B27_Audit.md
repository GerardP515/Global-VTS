## Classification

**No Hormuz Part I SRS.** Through traffic in B-IV/7–10 uses the IMO TSS plus national port-entry rules and voluntary security reports. Do not mint a VRS for UKMTO, JMIC or NCAGS.

**Bandar Abbas Port Control ≠ Hormuz VTS.** NGA text is: ships heading for *Iranian ports* report on passing Ras al Kuh. Transit-only ships are not captured by that sentence.

**Das VTIS ≠ coastal VTS.** ADNOC Petroleum Ports Authority procedure AHQ/CD/PPA/PRO/005 (Feb 2020) lists Zaqqum In/Out reporting points and states information is provided for vessels in the VTIS sector **including the Zaqqum TSS**. That is enough to associate Das VTIS with TSS-0135. It remains a field/port VTIS. Do not merge it with Hormuz.

**Tunb–Farur** is one IMO parent. Island claims affect who *enforces*, not how many TSS exist.
