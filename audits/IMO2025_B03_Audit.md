## Identity

B-I/11 is a multi-TSS parent. Inventory split is correct:

- TSS-0011 North Åland Sea
- TSS-0012 South Åland Sea

Fintraffic cites COLREG.2/Circ.60 Annex 2 for the Åland Sea TSS. Keep the two components separate. Do not merge with Archipelago VTS.

TSS-0014 is not TSS-0008. North and South Hoburgs Bank are different Part B parents.

## Mandatory reporting

None of these five schemes appear in IMO 2025 Part I.

Fintraffic, monitoring-international-waters page (retrieved 29 September 2026):

> The area does not have an obligatory reporting system for vessel traffic, but vessel traffic is monitored.

That sentence is attached to the Åland Sea TSS / South scheme page. It is positive evidence of **no** SOLAS V/11 scheme here, not a failed search.

Swedish recommended general calls (Transportstyrelsen TSS 2024-4663) for Klintehamn, Visby and Kalmarsund remain recommended coastal practice. They are not VRS records.

## Monitoring and VTS

### Åland Sea Traffic — TSS-0012 only

Authority: Fintraffic Vessel Traffic Services Ltd, Western Finland Vessel Traffic Centre.

Named service: **Åland Sea Traffic**.

Scope on the opened pages: **Southern Åland Sea TSS** only. Contact VHF 16. Monitoring by AIS. Proactive call before a COLREG violation. Violations reported to authorities. Deep-water route: deviation not allowed.

This is not:

- a Part I mandatory SRS;
- one of Fintraffic’s six coastal VTS areas (Bothnia, West Coast, Archipelago, Hanko, Helsinki, Kotka);
- proof that North Åland Sea is monitored by the same desk.

Classification: **confirmed associated monitoring service** for TSS-0012. No VTS-0xx until the project decides how to number monitoring-only services that sit outside designated VTS areas.

### TSS-0011 North Åland Sea

Not named on the Fintraffic South Åland Sea or international-waters pages. SMA material states Sweden Traffic monitors Swedish TSS other than Falsterborev / The Sound (those two sit with Sound VTS). That is COLREG Rule 10 monitoring, not a VTS area. Association: unresolved.

### TSS-0013 and TSS-0014

Same pattern as B02 South Hoburgs Bank / Midsjöbankarna. Nine current Swedish VTS areas do not include Gotland. Klintehamn reporting point 50 is inbound to the harbour.

### TSS-0015 Off Öland Island

Older NGA Pub. 194 describes VTS Kalmarsund (VHF 13, 300 GT / 45 m). Current Transportstyrelsen list and TSFS 2026:60 annex titles do not include a Kalmarsund VTS area. Treat historical Kalmarsund VTS as withdrawn or superseded unless a later official page restores it. Recommended general calls at Bredgrund and Trädgårdsgrund cover Kalmarsund transit, not the offshore IMO TSS south-east of Öland.
