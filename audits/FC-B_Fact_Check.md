## 1. Dangan Channel / VTS-0028 — qualified, not withdrawn

**What still stands**

- COLREG.2/Circ.71 places Dangan Channel East/West at about 22°06'–22°08'N, 114°06'–114°21'E (south of Waglan).
- HK Marine Department (Aug 2003 DCTSS paper): *“Parts of No.1 Precautionary area, No.1 Traffic lanes and the No.2 Precautionary area lie within the Hong Kong waters.”* HK VTC to inform vessels inside HKSAR; mainland authorities outside.
- HK NtM 97/2015 still charts the Pearl River routeing system against the HKSAR boundary.
- `VTS-0028` already exists. Reuse, do not mint.

**What does not stand as tightly as written**

- The 2025 HK VTS pamphlet / sector list names Eastern Approaches, Waglan as a calling-in point, Tathong and East Lamma. It does **not** say “Dangan Channel TSS”.
- The 2003 paper is older than the three-year rule. The 2015 NtM confirms the *routeing* system, not a fresh VTS-area polygon.
- Shenzhen VTS 2024 eastern line L7 is ~22°24.8'N. That is north of the Dangan lanes. Shenzhen is still not a link.

**Corrected label:** Existing service `VTS-0028`, coverage **Partial (HKSAR water only)**, evidence **medium**. Mainland water on the same TSS stays unresolved. Count impact still 0.

## 2. Nakhodka / +1 — kept, coverage still Partial

**What still stands**

- Rosmorport Far-Eastern pages (current): Nakhodka VTS, call sign Nakhodka-Traffic, Sectors 1B and 2 of Peter the Great Gulf Regional VTS.
- Sector 1B: approaches to Nakhodka Bay; S = territorial-sea limit; E 133°43.00'E; W 132°28.00'E.
- Sector 2: inside Nakhodka Gulf, S limit Cape Povorotny–Cape Likhachev.
- Not one of the 53. One identity, not two.

**What was too neat**

- NGA Pub. 155: the IMO TSS “lies in the approaches to Zaliv Nakhodka”. Separately, *“The VTS in Zaliv Nakhodka … comprises Sector 2 … operates 24 hours **N of** the Likhacheva–Povorotnyy line.”*
- Sector 2 is therefore the gulf, **north** of the entrance capes. The TSS is an *approaches* scheme, i.e. mainly **south** of that line.
- Coverage of the TSS therefore depends on Sector **1B**, not Sector 2. Rosmorport calls 1B “approaches” but does **not** print the words “traffic separation scheme”.
- TSS coordinates were not plotted on 1B.

**Corrected label:** New service Nakhodka VTS. Coverage **Partial** (1B inferred). Do not upgrade to Full until the 2025 book coordinates sit inside 1B. Count **+1** stands.

## 3. South Africa — both negatives hold

NGA Pub. 160, current wording: SA inshore VTS systems are *“distinct from an offshore system; i.e., for Laden Tankers off the Alphard Bank”*.

SANHO Annual 2026: two IMO TSS on Agulhas Bank, obligatory for laden tankers; reports may be passed via port control. That is tanker routeing, not a VTS identity on the TSS.

Mossel Bay VTS (SANHO appendix): radar ~20 NM, 12 NM and 6 NM reporting lines. FA platform TSS is 47 miles south. Geometry is not close.

No change.
