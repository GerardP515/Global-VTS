## Classification rules used

**TUBRAP ≠ Part I VRS.** Turkish Straits Reporting System is required by Turkish national rules and is printed in the TSVTS users’ guide next to the IMO TSS limits. It is not on the project Part I list (VRS-0001–VRS-0016 as loaded). Later Istanbul / Çanakkale / Marmara TSS in B25+ share the same TSVTS / TUBRAP pair. One national reporting system, many TSS. Do not mint VRS-00xx unless a Part I instrument is produced.

**TSVTS sectors are not separate VTS.** Istanbul VTS (Türkeli, Kandilli, Kadıköy, Marmara) and Çanakkale VTS are two sub-regions of one service family. Sector Türkeli is the north-approach working channel, not a new identity.

**Kerch / Crimea.** Record the IMO scheme. Record both claimant operators. Do not assign a VTS ID and do not treat either claim as a closed finding. The guide-scoping question is “how many distinct services would need an entry” — flag these two as high-maintenance unresolved, not as confirmed Ukrainian or Russian totals.

**Odesa group.** One IMO parent, three named Ukrainian VTS zones under one USPA/Delta Pilot family. Count one authority family for the publication estimate; keep the three zone names in the notes. Wartime corridor procedures (USPA 29 Jul 2022, VHF 69/73) are additional local instructions, not a second IMO SRS.
