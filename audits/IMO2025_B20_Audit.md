## Reporting instruments used

### VRS-0012 COPREP — MSC.278(85)

Title: Off the coast of Portugal [COPREP]. In force 1 June 2009. Report to **Roca Control** (VHF 22/79, secondary 69).

Geographic box (annex 2.1): between the Portuguese coast and 39°45'N to the north; a line through 39°45'N 010°14'W, 38°41'N 010°14'W, 36°30'N 009°35'W, 36°15'N 008°30'W to the west and south; 008°30'W to the east.

Cape St Vicente / Cabo de São Vicente lies near 37°01'N 008°59'W. The associated TSS sits inside that box. Association for TSS-0096: **confirmed by area**, not by scheme name in the COPREP annex.

Ships: 300 GT+; dangerous/HNS/polluting cargo; passenger ships; tow/push combined LOA >100 m; fishing vessels LOA ≥24 m.

### VRS-0013 GIBREP — MSC.300(87) amending the existing Strait system

Area: 005°58'.00 W to 005°15'.00 W. The annex states that this area **includes the amended TSS In the Strait of Gibraltar**.

- Westbound report to **Tarifa Traffic** at 005°15'W (VHF 10, supp. 67).
- Eastbound report to **Tangier Traffic** at 005°58'W (VHF 69, supp. 68).

Both centres monitor the TSS by radar and AIS. That is one VRS with two centres. Not two reporting schemes.

Ships: 300 GT+ and ships carrying hazardous / potentially polluting cargo (MSC.300(87) categories). Older NGA 50 m wording is superseded for project use by the IMO annex.

### Not Part I

Cabo de Gata: COLREG.2 amendment note — voluntary notification via Almería MRCC, VHF 16. Do not invent GATREP as VRS-00xx.

## VTS distinctions

- **Roca Control** = COPREP shore authority / Portuguese coastal VTS centre. Do not split into COPREP + a second identical VTS record without a separate legal VTS area instrument.
- **Tarifa Traffic** and **Tangier Traffic** = GIBREP centres. They also function as VTS/monitoring for the Strait TSS.
- **Gibraltar Port Authority VTS** = British Gibraltar Territorial Waters and port coordination (GPA, operational since 2011, VHF 12 in secondary lists). Not coverage of the IMO Strait TSS.
- **Algeciras Traffic** = Spanish port-approach VTS. Adjacent. Not the Strait TSS unless a later authority page says so.

No VTS-0xx issued from this batch.
