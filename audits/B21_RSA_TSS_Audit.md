---

## Batch rule reminder

- A port control tower is not a VTS covering a TSS unless the authority names the scheme or its approach as inside the service area.
- Field-construction traffic control is not automatically coverage of an IMO TSS used by passing tankers.
- VTIS / VTMS names are recorded as the authority uses them. They may still be a VTS under IMO Res. A.1158(32).
- Routine reports inside a VTS are not a separate reporting scheme.

Saudi Aramco 2025 port book: “The separation zones and routes shown on the charts of the Ras Tanura Channel are approved by IMO.” |
| Grouping note | Combined source heading. Older UKHO lists split “Approaches to Ra’s Tannūrah” and “Approaches to Ra’s al Ju’aymah” and noted a Ju’aymah departure channel. Retain the parent row. Do not count two TSS until *Ships’ Routeing* shows two adopted schemes. |

| Proposed relationship | TSS-0102 **covered_by** VTS-017 (provisional). |
| Guide-entry hint | Complex. Arrival channel, departure channel, Ju’aymah SPM/LPG branch, Dammam use of the same channel. At least two directional diagrams. |

**Sources**

- UKHO Annual Notice 17 (2026 listing), section (11).
- Saudi Aramco Ports and Terminals, July 2025: VTMS; IMO-approved Ras Tanura Channel routes; VHF 10/13 reports.
- NGA Pub. 172, Sector 16: IMO TSS for the three-terminal approach / departure system.
- SRC-001 candidate label.

**Unresolved:** whether Aramco’s VTMS meets the project definition of a VTS under A.1158(32), or is port VTMS only; whether Dammam has a separate service; ALRS Vol. 6 Saudi pages.
