## Mandatory reporting

### VRS-0001 GOFREP — TSS-0006 and TSS-0007

Same instruments as B01: MSC.139(76), MSC.231(82); Fintraffic Master’s Guide; Estonian Transpordiamet / Riigi Teataja VTS-GOFREP regulation.

The Central Reporting Line is drawn through the midpoints of the TSS off **Kõpu, Hankoniemi, Porkkala and Kalbådagrund**.

The Western Reporting Line starts from Kõpu Peninsula.

That is authority evidence for:

- TSS-0006 **within_reporting_scheme** VRS-0001 — confirmed
- TSS-0007 **within_reporting_scheme** VRS-0001 — confirmed

Tallinn Traffic monitors south of the Central Line and west of 026°30'E. Helsinki Traffic monitors north. Same three-centre GOFREP service as B01. Do not create a second reporting ID.

### TSS-0008 and TSS-0009

Not listed in IMO 2025 Part I. Closest Swedish reporting is Transportstyrelsen “recommendation regarding general call outside VTS areas” (TSS 2024-4663) for Gotland ports (Klintehamn, Visby, etc.). That is recommended coastal calling, not an IMO SRS and not tied to these two TSS.

### TSS-0010

Not in Part I. Bothnia VTS advance report at Nordvalen is a national VTS procedure for ships bound to Finnish Bothnian ports via the Quark, not a SOLAS V/11 scheme.

## VTS

### Hanko VTS vs TSS-0006

Fintraffic Master’s Guide (Hanko VTS area): the VTS covers “areas around Hankoniemi including the merchant shipping lanes in the area between Fläckgrund and Jussarö” plus Port of Hanko waters. Corner points run south to 59°40.5'N. The text does not name the IMO TSS. Hanko VTS ≠ Helsinki Traffic ≠ GOFREP.

Association: **adjacent / unresolved**.

### Estonian VTS vs TSS-0007

Transpordiamet: VTS area is Estonian waters from Kõpu Peninsula to Narva-Jõesuu. Western reporting line begins at Kõpu. GOFREP lies north of the VTS northern limit. Call sign for both western/eastern VTS and GOFREP south is Tallinn Traffic (VHF 61). Shared call sign is not two services counted as one, and is not proof that the Kõpu TSS sits inside the VTS polygon.

Association: **adjacent / unresolved**.

### Sweden vs TSS-0008 and TSS-0009

Transportstyrelsen / Sjöfartsverket designated VTS areas (nine): Luleå, Öregrund, Stockholm, Landsort, Mälaren, Bråviken, Göteborg, Marstrand, Lysekil. No Gotland or Hoburgs / Midsjö VTS. Do not invent one. Do not mark confirmed absent.

### Bothnia VTS vs TSS-0010

Fintraffic Bothnia VTS: ships bound for Finnish Gulf of Bothnia ports via the Quark submit an advance report 20 NM before Nordvalen Lighthouse (63°32.15'N 020°46.60'E). NGA Pub. 195 states Bothnia VTS includes routes to Vaasa and the main shipping routes in the southern part of Norra Kvarken. That is a strong lead. The opened Bothnia VTS Master’s Guide page does not say the IMO TSS polygon is inside the VTS area.

Swedish VTS Luleå is the northern Bothnian bay, not the Quark.

Association: **lead / unresolved**. No VTS-0xx issued.
