## Greece

Hellenic Coast Guard VTS contact page (opened 29 Sep 2026) names operating centres including **VTS ΠΕΙΡΑΙΑ**, VHF 13, 14, 15.

Thessaloniki: Wikipedia lists a VTS coordinate, but that is not an authority users’ page. Voria 25 September 2026 reports an ESPA “Transport 2021–2027” scheme that will **establish three new** traffic-control stations (Thessaloniki, Elefsina, Neapoli Voion) and upgrade seven existing hubs (Piraeus, Rafina, Lavrio, Patras, Rio-Antirrio, Corfu, Igoumenitsa). That wording treats Thessaloniki as **not yet** in the existing-hub set. Current-status tick: unresolved, not confirmed absent.

No Greek Mediterranean Part I SRS found.

## Egypt

NGA Pub. 132 (Damietta): TSS with west and east arms; “A Vessel Traffic Service is in operation in the port of Damietta”; Dumyat Port Control, VHF 14 and 16. Al-Mal 25 Jan 2024: Damietta Port Authority tendered a VTS out to 25 km from the shore, including waiting areas. Those two statements can both be true (existing port control plus a replacement system). Overlay of the 25 km circle on the IMO TSS arms is not done.

Port Said / Bur Said: SCA *Rules of Navigation* define SC-VTMS. NGA Pub. 172: radar about 19 miles off Port Said; Mediterranean arrivals call Port Said Harbor Office. Reporting is SCA canal procedure, not SOLAS V/11 Part I.
