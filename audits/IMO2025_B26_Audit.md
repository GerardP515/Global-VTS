## Identity traps

**Aqaba entrance ≠ Aqaba port.**

- TSS-0126 is the IMO scheme at the **entrance** (Strait of Tiran).
- Sailing directions: IMO TSS; station **VTS Gulf of Aqaba** monitors that TSS and 15 M N/S of the station (VHF 8, 9, 10, 16).
- **Aqaba VTS** (Jordan Maritime Commission / APMSCO): ALRS-style area is Jordanian waters from the Jordan/Saudi border to the Jordan/Israel border at the **head** of the gulf. Call Aqaba Port Control then Aqaba VTS. That service does not automatically cover Tiran.

**Jazan ≠ Jeddah / King Abdullah.** Separate Saudi Red Sea ports. B-IV/3 is the Jazan Economic City / JPDI deep-water route the 2025 book now carries. Keep it in the inventory; it was the v0.2 gap.

**UKMTO / MSTC ≠ Part I.** The Maritime Security Transit Corridor joins the IRTC, the Bab el Mandeb TSS and the TSS west of Hanish. Reporting to UKMTO and NAVCENT is voluntary security practice. Do not mint VRS-00xx.

## Jazan evidence

Aramco *Jazan Refinery Marine Terminals* notes, July 2025: main arrival/departure channel developed and approved by IMO MSC and NCSR, about 90 NM, intended for all vessels and particularly VLCC to the SPM. That supports the book identity of TSS-0127. Hutchison Ports Jazan operates JCPDI Port. No opened Mawani / RCJY VTS manual naming the TSS.
