## Evidence check

### TSS-0021 — Off Falsterborev

IMO Part B directly points to the mandatory reporting system **SOUNDREP**.

The Swedish Maritime Administration states that SOUNDREP is operated by **Sound VTS**. The Royal Danish Navy's current VTS page defines SOUNDREP Sector 2 as extending south and east of the traffic separation scheme at Falsterbo lighthouse.

The current Danish Maritime Authority routeing guide also presents Off Falsterborev immediately with the SOUNDREP operational-area material.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0022 — In The Sound

IMO Part B directly points to SOUNDREP. IMO MSC.314(88) names the TSS in the adopted system.

Sound VTS operates SOUNDREP from Malmö. The TSS lies in the northern part of the Sound operational area, corresponding to SOUNDREP Sector 1.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0023 — North of Rügen

The current BSH VTS Centre Warnemünde overview shows the German Baltic VTS sectors around Rügen. **Stralsund Traffic** covers the waters approaching the northern tip of Rügen from the west, while Sassnitz Traffic starts east of the Cape Arkona boundary.

The IMO North of Rügen TSS lies west of Cape Arkona. Its published longitudes are approximately 013°03'E to 013°14'E, placing it in the Stralsund Traffic sector.

No IMO Part I mandatory reporting system applies.

Classification: **VTS only**.

### TSS-0024 — South of Gedser

The TSS is part of the operational Kadetrenden corridor. Current BSH material identifies **Kadetrenden Traffic** as a VTS sector of VTS Centre Warnemünde, while current Danish Maritime Authority guidance treats TSS South of Gedser and the Kadetrenden deep-water route as one connected routeing area.

No IMO Part I mandatory reporting system applies. AIS/listening and VTS procedures are not counted as a separate MRS.

Classification: **VTS only**.

### TSS-0025 — Off Kiel lighthouse

Current German traffic regulations identify **Kiel Traffic**, operated from VTS Centre Travemünde, on VHF 67.

Applicable vessels must make a pre-entry report before passing Kiel Lighthouse and a position report when passing the lighthouse. The current BSH VTS overview confirms the Kiel Traffic service area at the entrance to Kieler Förde.

This directly connects the TSS at Kiel Lighthouse with the operational VTS.

No IMO Part I mandatory reporting system applies.

Classification: **VTS only**.
