## Record detail

### TSS-0141 Off Mina Al-Ahmadi – South scheme

This is one of three schemes adopted as 'Off Mina Al-Ahmadi' (COLREG.2/Circ.55, MSC 79, December 2004). Current official sailing directions describe port control and a security zone administered from the Mina al Ahmadi control tower, not a VTS. No Kuwait Ports Authority or KOC VTS source naming the scheme was found.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2077.

Unresolved: Whether Kuwait (KPA or KOC) operates a VTS or monitoring service covering the Mina Al-Ahmadi schemes; ALRS Vol. 6; security-zone limits against the scheme geometry.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved; the control tower is port or security control, not a VTS.

### TSS-0142 Off Dondra Head

The scheme was adopted by MSC 42 (1980; COLREG.2/Circ.14) and remains in force. The MEPC 80/16/3 industry submission (2023) seeks a new TSS further south. Official sources opened do not link any VTS or reporting scheme to it, but no official complete list of Sri Lankan VTS or MRS was found, so absence cannot be recorded.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2081.

Unresolved: Sri Lanka Ports Authority / Merchant Shipping Secretariat / Sri Lanka Navy statement on any monitoring of the TSS; the adoption instrument edition is not rechecked; ALRS Vol. 6 for Sri Lanka.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: first-pass 'Neither confirmed' changed to Unresolved; no authoritative evidence of absence (no complete official list).

### TSS-0143 Off Alphard Banks 34 miles south of Cape Infanta

The scheme was adopted by MSC 69 (COLREG.2/Circ.44, in force 1 December 1998). Pub. 171 (2026) gives routeing advice only: laden tankers keep well clear and avoid within 6 miles of the shallowest part of Alphard Banks, and an oil development area lies to the east. The first-pass 'offshore laden-tanker system' is routeing guidance, not a VTS or reporting scheme. SAFREP covers the area but SAMSA states that participation is voluntary (MN 35 of 2018), so it is not an MRS.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2084; SRC-2089; SRC-2090.

Unresolved: No VTS or mandatory reporting found, but absence is not proven. Whether SAMSA or MRCC Cape Town monitors the TSS (MRCC is not a VTS under decision 1). The MN 6 of 1999 text itself was not retrieved; its voluntary status rests on the later valid MN 35 of 2018.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: stays Unresolved but the lead's 'offshore laden-tanker system' is rejected as a VTS or MRS; it is routeing advice in Pub. 171.

### TSS-0144 Off the FA platform 47 miles south of Mossel Bay

The scheme was adopted by MSC 69 (COLREG.2/Circ.44, 1998) around the FA platform, within an offshore oil development and safety-zone area (Pub. 171). There is no evidence of a VTS or TSS-specific reporting. Mossel Bay port services stop at the 12-mile reporting line. SAFREP covers the area but SAMSA states that participation is voluntary (MN 35 of 2018), so it is not an MRS. Absence of any VTS or MRS is not proven.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2084; SRC-2089; SRC-2090.

Unresolved: Whether the FA platform and scheme remain in use. Any SAMSA or TNPA monitoring of the TSS. No mandatory reporting found, but absence is not proven.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: first-pass 'Neither confirmed' changed to Unresolved; no official complete list of South African VTS or MRS was found.

### TSS-0145 At One Fathom Bank

The TSS is wholly inside the STRAITREP operational area and within Sectors 1 and 2, both assigned to Klang VTS. MSC.73(69) and the current MPA sector table name Klang VTS; Pub. 174 (2025) confirms STRAITREP is in operation. VTS-0004 and VRS-0016 are reused. No new entity.

VTS sources: SRC-2086; SRC-2087; SRC-2079. Reporting sources: SRC-2086; SRC-2087; SRC-2088.

Unresolved: The exact Sector 1/2 boundary latitude is read from a small-scale chartlet (about 02 52 N); it does not affect the result because both sectors are Klang VTS. A Marine Department Malaysia primary page for Klang VTS was not opened; current operation rests on MPA (2026) and Pub. 174 (2025).

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: VTS + MRS with VTS-0004 and VRS-0016; boundary basis added from MSC.73(69), the MPA chartlet and Circ.44.

## Method notes

Research 30 September 2026. The Aramco site blocks curl (Akamai 403 / HTTP2 reset), so the July 2025 chapters were retrieved via WebFetch and text was extracted locally with pypdf. The 'ports-and-terminals-2025' and '2025-update' URLs now return 404. NGA Pubs 171 (2026), 172 (2025), 173 (2026) and 174 (2025) were downloaded from msi.nga.mil and searched in full. IMO instruments: A.338(IX) was downloaded from IMO. The COLREG.2 circular compilation (Transportstyrelsen) and MSC.73(69) were read from copies already in the shared scratchpad, which are byte-identical to the Transportstyrelsen download made this session. mandatory_reporting 'No' for TSS-0136 means no Part I scheme and only VTS routine reporting was found. 'Unresolved' elsewhere means national-level absence is not proven. Voluntary reporting 'No' for Gulf rows means no TSS-specific voluntary scheme; regional voluntary security reporting (UKMTO/JMIC) was not assessed and is not an MRS. First-pass findings were treated as leads: 0136 corrected (partial VTS), 0142 and 0144 corrected to Unresolved, the 0143 lead rejected, and 0145 confirmed. No repository files were edited.
