## Record detail

### TSS-0116 Eastern approach to Bur Said

The IMO eastern approach TSS is not named in the SCA Rules of Navigation (December 2020 edition). SC-VTMS tracking is described only as 'SC approaches (15 miles faraway)'. Being close to the service is not coverage, and the only source dates from 2020. The Port Said arrival call is an SCA port/canal procedure. EAMS VTIMS covers Alexandria (25 NM), not Port Said.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2048; SRC-2049.

Unresolved: Need a current (2023 or later) official SCA or EAMS source that names the eastern approach TSS or gives an SC-VTMS / Port Said port-control boundary covering it, plus the TSS coordinates to overlay against the 15-mile tracking description. Also confirm whether any Egyptian national reporting requirement applies seaward of the Fairway Buoy.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: the lead's 'same SCA SC-VTMS as TSS-0115' is not established for this TSS. The one SC-VTMS identity is kept as a lead only.

### TSS-0117 In the southern approaches to the Kerch Strait

Rosmorport, a Russian Federation operator, publishes a Kerch Strait VTS (Kavkaz centre, Kerch sub-centre) whose area polygon has a southern limit of 44°59.75'N. Whether the IMO TSS falls inside is not shown. The area is disputed: Ukraine's position, including its communications to IMO (e.g. Circular Letter 4508, not opened in this pass), is recorded neutrally. Nothing here is evidence of current safe operation.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2048; SRC-2051.

Unresolved: Overlay the current IMO B-III/15 TSS coordinates on the claimed polygon (the 2003 NP 24 lead suggests the TSS extends south of it, so partial at most). Project decision needed on how to record services claimed by an operator whose jurisdiction in this area is disputed. The IMO Circular Letter 4508 text was not retrieved.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: kept Unresolved with no operator assigned. Refined with the published polygon southern limit.

### TSS-0118 In the area off the south-western coast of the Crimea

The TSS was implemented in 2010 (COLREG.2/Circ.61, per the first-pass lead; not reopened). No current official VTS source covering the TSS was found. The historical Ukrainian Sevastopol VTS is not evidence of current operation. The area is disputed; authority statements are recorded neutrally and nothing here asserts current safe operation.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2048.

Unresolved: Current operator and status of any VTS covering the Sevastopol approaches. Record claims of the parties neutrally if the project decides to track them.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved, current operator unknown.

### TSS-0119 Approaches to the Chornomorsk, Odesa and Pivdennyi ports

The combined TSS is Ukraine's proposal merging the 1981 schemes (COLREG.2/Circ.14), now B-III/17 (COLREG.2/Circ.78, in force 1 June 2023, per the region note). The Delta-Lotsman VTS (USPA) is plainly involved in these approaches, but the only operational source opened is dated 2022, and no official VTS area overlay was found. Operations are conflict-affected; no statement on safe operation is made.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2048; SRC-2052.

Unresolved: Need a 2023 or later USPA/Delta-Lotsman source, or the current VTS zone limits (Order 655/2007 or its successor), showing VTCP Odesa / Chornomorsk / Pivdennyi coverage of the TSS. Then decide on one service identity with three zones.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed as a lead only: kept Unresolved under decision 4 (latest official operational source is 2022).

### TSS-0120 Strait of Istanbul – North approach

The 2019 Regulation names the north approach TSS (Annex 1.1) as part of the Turkish Straits TSS that TBGTH is established to implement and supervise (Art. 5). The 2023 Implementation Directive confirms TBGTH Centres are current. TÜBRAP (national) applies on this approach. No VTS sector polygon was obtained, so coverage rests on the statutory link and the approach reporting duties.

VTS sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2046; SRC-2047; SRC-2056. Reporting sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2048.

Unresolved: The TSVTS User's Guide sector limits (seaward limit of the Istanbul VTS area in the north approach) were not obtained. Coverage is recorded as full on the statutory basis; confirm with the sector chart.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: VTS confirmed from statutory sources (Art. 5 and Annex 1.1), not the user guide. Sector Türkeli is unverified. TÜBRAP confirmed absent from the project Part I baseline and counted as a national MRS.
