## Method notes

Accessed 30 September 2026. Geometry: TSS coordinates from the COLREG.2 circulars (Circ.54, Circ.58, Circ.51, Circ.60, A.338(IX)) compared with official VTS limit coordinates and the VTS area charts in the Koper, Trieste and Venice manuals (images rendered and inspected). Circular coordinates may have been amended before the 2025 Ships' Routeing edition; the baseline edition itself was not reopened. ADRIREP: MSC.139(76) Part I text is in force until 30 Nov 2026 and MSC.598(111) replaces it from 1 Dec 2026 with the same area (north of 40°25'N); VRS-0015 applies to both. Greek and Egyptian official sites returned 403 or connection resets (hcg.gr, hellenicparliament.gr, ynanp.gr, dpa.gov.eg); NGA MSI API rejected requests; B23 therefore remains Unresolved. Italian manuals are the editions currently published on the Guardia Costiera portal (2017/2018 text, files re-uploaded Sep 2024); the VTS Trieste link (0110) carries a decision 4 currency flag. No VTS IDs allocated; Koper VTS and VTS Trieste are new entities not in the existing register. Press items (Al-Mal 2024, Qatar News Agency July 2026) used only as leads.
