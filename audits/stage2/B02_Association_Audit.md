## Evidence check

### TSS-0006 — Off Hankoniemi Peninsula

GOFREP is confirmed. IMO and current Fintraffic material place the reporting-system geometry through the Hankoniemi TSS.

Hanko VTS is not the same operational area. Current Fintraffic gives its southern boundary at latitude 59°40.5'N. The IMO TSS lies much farther south, between about 59°24.5'N and 59°30.0'N. The TSS is therefore outside the current Hanko VTS area.

Classification: **MRS only**.

### TSS-0007 — Off Kõpu Peninsula

GOFREP is confirmed. Current Estonian material explicitly separates the VTS area from the GOFREP area and describes entry to Western VTS by crossing the western reporting line that begins at Kõpu peninsula.

The authority material does not establish the offshore Kõpu TSS itself as lying inside Western VTS. The VTS relationship is therefore not asserted.

Classification: **MRS only**.

### TSS-0008 — South Hoburgs Bank

The current Swedish Maritime Administration VTS directory lists the operational Swedish VTS areas. There is no Gotland/Hoburgs Bank VTS area. No applicable IMO Part I mandatory reporting scheme covers this TSS.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0009 — Midsjöbankarna

The same current Swedish national VTS directory provides no VTS area at Midsjöbankarna. No applicable IMO Part I mandatory reporting scheme covers the TSS.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0010 — In Norra Kvarken

Current Traficom VTS-area coordinates resolve the earlier Bothnia VTS lead. Bothnia VTS begins at Ritgrund lighthouse, 63°25.5'N 021°30.5'E, then runs to the Finnish territorial-sea boundary at 63°40.0'N 021°31.0'E and northwards.

The IMO Norra Kvarken TSS is west of this boundary, with its parts lying approximately between 020°37'E and 021°04'E. It is therefore outside Bothnia VTS.

Fintraffic requires certain Finland-bound vessels travelling via the Quark to submit an advance report 20 nautical miles before Nordvalen. This is an approach/reporting procedure before entry to the VTS and is not evidence that the TSS is inside Bothnia VTS.

The Swedish national VTS directory lists no Swedish Kvarken VTS. No applicable IMO Part I mandatory reporting scheme covers the TSS.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.
