## Evidence check

### TSS-0031 — Lilla Middelgrund

The IMO TSS lies at roughly 56°55'N to 56°57'N.

MSC.314(88) defines the northern boundary of SOUNDREP at approximately 56°18'N. Lilla Middelgrund is therefore well north of the Sound VTS/SOUNDREP operational area.

Current Danish and Swedish VTS listings do not identify a separate Lilla Middelgrund or offshore Kattegat VTS.

No other applicable IMO Part I mandatory reporting system was identified.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0032 — Entrance to The Sound

This case is direct.

Denmark and Sweden's NCSR 10/INF.7 states that TSS **Entrance to the Sound** is situated within the mandatory ship reporting system **SOUNDREP**, operated by **Sound VTS**.

Current SOUNDREP procedures confirm that Sound Traffic operates the reporting area in two sectors. The TSS lies within the northern sector.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0033 — West of the Scilly Isles

IMO Part B B-II/1 expressly directs mariners to the **West European Tanker Reporting System**.

MCA MGN 610 Amendment 1 confirms that WETREP remains a mandatory IMO ship reporting system and that its reporting area extends from southern Portugal to the Shetland Islands.

No operational VTS relationship was established for this offshore TSS.

The MCA separately lists a voluntary reporting scheme in the Isles of Scilly. Exact TSS-level coverage of that voluntary scheme was not established in this pass and is recorded separately from the mandatory classification.

Classification: **MRS only**.

### TSS-0034 — South of the Scilly Isles

IMO Part B B-II/2 contains the same direct WETREP reference. Current MCA guidance confirms WETREP remains mandatory.

No operational VTS relationship was established.

The voluntary Isles of Scilly reporting scheme is a separate lead; its exact boundary relative to this TSS remains unresolved and does not affect the WETREP classification.

Classification: **MRS only**.

### TSS-0035 — Off Land's End, between Seven Stones and Longships

IMO Part B B-II/3 expressly refers to WETREP. MCA guidance confirms the current mandatory system and its broad western-European reporting area.

No operational VTS relationship was established for the offshore Land's End TSS.

Classification: **MRS only**.

## Duplicate check

- TSS-0032 shares the existing **VTS-0003 Sound VTS** and **VRS-0003 SOUNDREP**.
- TSS-0033, TSS-0034 and TSS-0035 share **VRS-0005 WETREP**.
- WETREP is one reporting scheme, not one service per coastal State.
- The UK voluntary Isles of Scilly reporting scheme is not merged into WETREP and is not counted as mandatory.
- No coastguard station is relabelled as a VTS.
