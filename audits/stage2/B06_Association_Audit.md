## Evidence check

### TSS-0026 — Between Korsoer and Sprogoe

IMO MSC.332(90) states that BELTREP is operated by **Great Belt VTS** and defines its operational area across the central and northern Great Belt.

The same resolution has a dedicated section 6.4 for the TSS **Between Korsoer and Sprogoe**, placing it inside the BELTREP operational framework. Current Danish guidance also places this TSS adjacent to the mapped BELTREP / Great Belt VTS area and directs vessels to Great Belt VTS.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0027 — At Hatter Barn

MSC.332(90) expressly states that the BELTREP operational area includes the **Hatter Barn area** and has a dedicated section for TSS At Hatter Barn.

Current Danish guidance states that the mandatory BELTREP ship reporting system, operated by Great Belt VTS, covers the Great Belt and Hatter Barn areas.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0028 — Skagen West

The current Royal Danish Navy VTS page identifies Denmark's operational VTS services as:
- Sound VTS;
- Great Belt VTS;
- Fehmarnbelt VTS.

It identifies no Skagen VTS. Current Danish routeing material describes Skagen West as part of the Kattegat/Skagerrak routeing system rather than a VTS area.

No applicable IMO Part I mandatory reporting scheme covers Skagen West.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0029 — Skagen East

The same current Danish VTS and routeing sources provide no Skagen VTS service and no mandatory ship reporting system for Skagen East.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0030 — Fladen

The IMO TSS lies around 57°13'N to 57°15'N.

Sjöfartsverket's current Göteborg VTS boundary has a southern limit at 57°31.9'N, with the western boundary formed by a six-mile arc centred on Vinga. Fladen is therefore well outside Göteborg VTS.

The Swedish national VTS directory identifies no separate offshore Fladen/Kattegat VTS. No applicable IMO Part I mandatory reporting scheme covers the TSS.

A Göteborg VTS boundary amendment enters into force on 1 October 2026 and moves the southern limit only to about 57°30.6'N. Fladen remains far outside the area.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.
