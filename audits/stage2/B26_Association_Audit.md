## Record detail

### TSS-0126 In the entrance to the Gulf of Aqaba

Pub. 172 (2025 ed., corrected to July 2026): 'A Vessel Traffic Service Station (call sign: VTS Gulf of Aqaba) ... Ensure safety of navigation within the Traffic Separation Scheme of the Gulf of Aqaba', also monitoring 15 miles N and S of the station; separate Nabq radar station 'Salam' gives local information. Aqaba VTS (Jordan, APMSCO) covers only Jordanian waters at the head of the gulf and is not linked. Operator and current operation of 'VTS Gulf of Aqaba' not confirmed by any authority source; EAMS (Egypt) page naming Tiran Straits returned 404 (snippet only).

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: none.

Unresolved: Identify the operating authority (Egypt EAMS/MTS or other) and current service area of 'VTS Gulf of Aqaba'; obtain a coastal-State or ALRS Vol 6 entry. Would move to VTS only if the authority is confirmed. Check whether UKMTO/MSCIO VRA voluntary reporting extends to the Strait of Tiran.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved kept; Pub. 172 text re-read, but the station's operator and currency are unconfirmed, so it remains a lead and is not merged with Aqaba VTS.

### TSS-0127 Near the deep-water route leading to Jazan Economic City Port

Pub. 172 (2025 ed., corrected to July 2026): 'An IMO-adopted Deep-Water Route ... leads to the port; an IMO-adopted Traffic Separation Scheme is located at the seaward end of the Deep-Water Route.' Contacts are port-level only. Aramco Jazan terminal PDFs (2025 update) could not be retrieved (403/stream errors) and were not used; Mawani site gave no VTS page.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: none.

Unresolved: Obtain Mawani / Jazan port or Aramco Jazan terminal regulations showing whether any port traffic centre area includes the TSS at the seaward end of the deep-water route (about 90 miles offshore).

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved; Port Control remains a lead, no VTS area source found.

### TSS-0128 In the southern Red Sea – west and south of Hanish al Kubra

Pub. 172 identifies the TSS W and S of Jazirat al Hanish al Kabir and places the MSTC 'through areas SW of Jazirat al Hanish al Kabir'. No VTS or mandatory reporting described. Conflict-affected area: IMO adoption is not evidence of current safe operation.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2066.

Unresolved: No authoritative list of Yemeni or Eritrean VTS/MRS located; absence cannot be certified.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: first pass 'Neither confirmed' changed to Unresolved, because no authoritative evidence of absence exists; UKMTO correctly kept voluntary.

### TSS-0129 In the southern Red Sea – east of Jabal Zuqar Island

Pub. 172 identifies the TSS E of Jazirat Jabal Zuqar (recommended for northbound traffic). Unlike TSS-0128 and TSS-0130, it is not named in Pub. 172's MSTC description, so voluntary MSTC/UKMTO applicability is not shown by the sources opened. Conflict-affected area.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: none.

Unresolved: No authoritative Yemeni VTS/MRS list; confirm UKMTO/MSCIO VRA extent for this TSS.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: 'Neither confirmed' changed to Unresolved (no evidence of absence); separate TSS from TSS-0128, not merged.

### TSS-0130 In the Strait of Bab el Mandeb

Pub. 172: 'An IMO-adopted Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) lies in the waters of Bab al Mandeb'; the MSTC runs 'through Bab el Mandeb'. No VTS or MRS described. Conflict-affected area; security arrangements recorded neutrally.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2066.

Unresolved: Whether any Yemeni, Djiboutian or Eritrean service claims coverage of the strait; no authoritative national lists located.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: 'Neither confirmed' changed to Unresolved; security reporting kept voluntary.

Coverage for TSS-0135 rests on the ADNOC statement and reporting-point positions, not a lane-coordinate overlay. Conflict-affected areas (southern Red Sea, Bab el Mandeb, Hormuz) recorded neutrally; 2026 IMO/Oman statements that the Hormuz TSS should not be used are recorded as context, not as removal of the TSS. The UAE claim to the Tunb islands is recorded neutrally. No repository files edited.
