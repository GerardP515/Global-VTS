## Record detail

### TSS-0136 In the approaches to Ras Tanura and Ju’aymah

Aramco's July 2025 port book describes a mandatory VTMS with a 'VTS Operator' information service. It covers all vessels within the Port of Ras Tanura and states that the Ras Tanura Channel separation zones and routes are IMO-approved. Speed and reporting rules apply to 'all ships using the Ras Tanura Port, Traffic Separation Scheme'. The port boundary includes the channel and Ju'aymah parts of the TSS but not the eastern approach lanes between about 50 23 E and 50 42 E. Pub. 172 (2025) confirms the VTMS covers Ras Tannurah and the Ju Aymah terminals and lists reporting points along the approach. It is an official port traffic management service (decision 5), with partial coverage.

VTS sources: SRC-2075; SRC-2076; SRC-2077; SRC-2078. Reporting sources: SRC-2075; SRC-2077.

Unresolved: The current IMO 2025 geometry has not been checked against A.338(IX) (1975, 'as amended'); later amendments may move the eastern end. VTMS coverage of the eastern lanes beyond the port limit rests only on reporting points, not on a defined service area.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: the first pass left this Unresolved. The Aramco July 2025 harbour boundary and VTMS text, with the A.338(IX) geometry, establish partial VTS coverage (VTS only, partial). No VTS ID existed in the register.

### TSS-0137 Marjan/Zuluf

The IMO scheme lies between Zuluf field (W) and the Marjan and Maharah fields (E) (Pub. 172 16.33). No VTS, port control or reporting requirement is stated for passing traffic. The Aramco offshore field marine traffic control centre is recorded as a lead only; no official source for it was opened this session.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2077.

Unresolved: Whether a Saudi Aramco offshore marine traffic control centre (Marjan field) monitors the TSS or requires calls from through traffic; an ALRS Vol. 6 entry is needed.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: remains Unresolved; the MTCC is still an unverified lead.

### TSS-0138 Approaches to the port of Ra’s al Khafji

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2077.

Unresolved: Al-Khafji Joint Operations or Saudi port authority traffic service status; ALRS Vol. 6 entry for Ras al Khafji.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved; the terminal VHF 16 is not a VTS.

## Method notes

Research 30 September 2026. The Aramco site blocks curl (Akamai 403 / HTTP2 reset), so the July 2025 chapters were retrieved via WebFetch and text was extracted locally with pypdf. The 'ports-and-terminals-2025' and '2025-update' URLs now return 404. NGA Pubs 171 (2026), 172 (2025), 173 (2026) and 174 (2025) were downloaded from msi.nga.mil and searched in full. IMO instruments: A.338(IX) was downloaded from IMO. The COLREG.2 circular compilation (Transportstyrelsen) and MSC.73(69) were read from copies already in the shared scratchpad, which are byte-identical to the Transportstyrelsen download made this session. mandatory_reporting 'No' for TSS-0136 means no Part I scheme and only VTS routine reporting was found. 'Unresolved' elsewhere means national-level absence is not proven. Voluntary reporting 'No' for Gulf rows means no TSS-specific voluntary scheme; regional voluntary security reporting (UKMTO/JMIC) was not assessed and is not an MRS. First-pass findings were treated as leads: 0136 corrected (partial VTS), 0142 and 0144 corrected to Unresolved, the 0143 lead rejected, and 0145 confirmed. No repository files were edited.
