## Record detail

### TSS-0131 Off Ras al Hadd

Pub. 172: 'An IMO-adopted Traffic Separation Scheme (TSS) ... lies in the waters off Ras al Hadd', with an Inshore Traffic Zone. No VTS or reporting scheme described.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2071.

Unresolved: Obtain an official Omani list of VTS (MTCIT, ASYAD ports, Oman Maritime Security Centre) to confirm or exclude coverage.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved; Omani port VTS remain leads only.

### TSS-0132 Off Ra’s al Kuh

Pub. 172: 'All vessels heading for Iranian ports should report to Bandar Abbas Port Control on passing Ras al Kuh ... stating their ETA at the Strait of Hormuz and their destination.' This may be a national port-entry reporting requirement but rests on a foreign sailing direction; in 2026 Iranian ports are subject to a declared naval blockade (LISCR MSB 07/2026 Rev.3), so current operation is uncertain. Recorded neutrally.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2066.

Unresolved: Locate an Iranian (PMO) instrument or ALRS entry for the Ras al Kuh report and any Iranian VTS area covering the TSS; decide under Decision 3 whether a port-bound-only report counts as a national scheme.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved; Port Control is not a VTS for this TSS; reporting reclassified from 'destination rule' to an unresolved national-scheme candidate.

### TSS-0133 In the Strait of Hormuz

IMO: TSS 'proposed by Iran and Oman and adopted by IMO in 1968'; IMO FAQ: 'The TSS should not be used due to the reported presence of mines.' Oman NHO warning (23 June 2026): 'CURRENT REPORTS INDICATE THAT THE TSS IS NOT SAFE FOR USE AT THIS TIME', with temporary routes N and S of the TSS. JMIC: NCAGS coordination 'is not mandatory'. IMO lists Iranian port VTS centres as contacts, without area coverage of the TSS. Recorded neutrally; IMO adoption is not evidence of current safe operation.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2069; SRC-2066; SRC-2073.

Unresolved: Scheme currently not in normal use; status of any Omani or Iranian VTS/monitoring covering the TSS once the emergency ends; whether any Iranian permit/reporting regime is formally promulgated.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: 'Neither confirmed' changed to Unresolved; 2026 suspension context added; no Part I Hormuz scheme confirmed.

### TSS-0134 Tunb–Farur

Pub. 172: one IMO TSS 'passes N and S of Jazireh-ye Tomb-e Bozorg and Jazireh-ye Forur'. No VTS or reporting scheme tied to it. Island sovereignty claims do not create a second scheme.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2066; SRC-2073.

Unresolved: Iranian VTS or reporting coverage of the TSS not documented; conflict-related 2026 measures may affect use.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: Unresolved; one IMO parent, sovereignty dispute not a second TSS.

### TSS-0135 Between Zaqqum and Umm Shaif

ADNOC PPA VTIS External Procedures (Rev. 01, effective 20 Feb 2020, still hosted by PPA): VTIS for the PPA-VTIS Sectors including 'the Zaqqum TSS'; 10.3 refers to the IMO Zaqqum TSS (limits not reproduced in the text layer); 10.8 Das/Zirku sector limits; 10.8.3/10.8.4 Zaqqum In/Out reporting points; section 12 navigational assistance 'within the VTIS Sector including the Zaqqum TSS'. Current PPA pages (copyright 2026): Das VTIS 'operates 24/7 on VHF channel 23' and 'currently operates only as an information system'; limits 'as per the VTIS external procedures'; 'The TSS must be followed by all vessels ... as per the instructions received from Das VTIS'. Marine notice DZM/MN/016/2026 (28 Sep 2026) directs a listening watch on 'VHF Channel 23 (Das VTIS)'. Pub. 172 (July 2026) describes the Das VTIS, its Das/Zirku sector (2020 limits) and the Zaqqum channel reporting points.

VTS sources: SRC-2060; SRC-2061; SRC-2062; SRC-2063; SRC-2064; SRC-2066; SRC-2074. Reporting sources: SRC-2060.

Unresolved: Coverage recorded as full on the 2020 procedure limits, which the current PPA page adopts; the PPA VTIS web page gives slightly shorter eastern points F/G that place the TSS eastern terminus on or marginally outside the boundary. Confirm with the IMO B-IV/11 coordinates or ENC if precision is needed. New VTS identity needed (not in existing register).

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: Unresolved changed to VTS only; the 2020 procedure is now backed by current (2026) official PPA pages and notices, satisfying Decision 4; the 2023 temporary notice is historical only.
