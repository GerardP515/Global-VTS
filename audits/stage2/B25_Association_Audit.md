## Record detail

### TSS-0121 Strait of Istanbul

The Istanbul Strait TSS (Annex 1.2) is the core of the TBGTH service area. The service has operated since 30 December 2003 from the İstinye centre (KEGM) and the 2023 Directive confirms current operation. Calling-point and position reports under TÜBRAP apply in the strait.

VTS sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2046; SRC-2047; SRC-2056. Reporting sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2048.

Unresolved: Sector limits and names not verified from a current official source.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: one TSVTS identity. Sectors are not separate services.

### TSS-0122 Strait of Istanbul – South approach and Sea of Marmara

KEGM expressly states that TBGTH coverage was extended in 2008 to include the Sea of Marmara TSS, giving monitoring of the whole Turkish Straits. The Regulation's Annex 1.3 is this TSS. TÜBRAP applies, including MARRAP for active participants in the Sea of Marmara and SP-1 for departures from Marmara ports.

VTS sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2046; SRC-2047; SRC-2056. Reporting sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2048.

Unresolved: Sector limits not verified. The separate İzmit VTS (KEGM, east of the Pendik Burnu–Deveboynu Burnu line) is a lead only: whether any part of this TSS also lies in the İzmit VTS area was not assessed. Do not create a separate 'Marmara VTS'.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: VTS coverage confirmed (the KEGM statement names the Marmara TSS). MRS confirmed as a national scheme (TÜBRAP incl. MARRAP). No separate Marmara VTS identity.

### TSS-0123 Strait of Çanakkale

The Çanakkale Strait TSS (Annex 1.4) is within the original TBGTH service, served by the Akbaş centre and operated by KEGM since 2003. TÜBRAP position and calling-point reports apply.

VTS sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2046; SRC-2047; SRC-2056. Reporting sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2048.

Unresolved: Sector limits and names not verified from a current official source.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: second centre of the same TSVTS service. Sectors are not separate VTS.

### TSS-0124 Strait of Çanakkale – South-west approach

The south-west approach TSS is Annex 1.5 of the 2019 Regulation, which TBGTH is established to supervise. KEGM added a Bozcaada surveillance station in 2008, and TÜBRAP approach reports go to the Çanakkale TBGTH Centre. No official sector chart was obtained.

VTS sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2046; SRC-2047; SRC-2056. Reporting sources: SRC-2044; SRC-2045; SRC-2048.

Unresolved: The seaward limit of the Çanakkale VTS area was not obtained from the TSVTS User's Guide. Coverage is recorded as full on the statutory basis; confirm with the sector chart.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: coverage rests on the statutory Annex 1.5 link and the Bozcaada station, not on the unverified 'south limit near 39°44'N 025°47'E'.

### TSS-0125 In the Gulf of Suez

The current EAMS website (language file last modified 23 September 2026; lists Gulf of Suez warnings to 4/2026 and a 2024/2025 investment plan item 'Radar monitoring and control (VTS)') describes GOS VTIMS as operated by EAMS since 2002 to serve vessel traffic across the Gulf of Suez, with VTS stations at El-Adabiya, Ras Ghareb, Safaga and Sharm El-Sheikh. It is distinct from the SCA SC-VTMS.

VTS sources: SRC-2050; SRC-2059. Reporting sources: SRC-2048.

Unresolved: No boundary polygon or official chart. The coverage finding rests on the gulf-wide service description. The Egyptian participation rule (NGA Pub. 172 lead) and any national reporting scheme remain to be checked. Also check whether SC-VTMS Suez anchorage tracking overlaps the northern end of the TSS.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: GOS VTIMS (EAMS) is distinct from SCA SC-VTMS, now supported by the current EAMS site.
