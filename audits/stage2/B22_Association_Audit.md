## Record detail

### TSS-0106 In the Adriatic Sea: approaches to Gulf of Trieste

Inside ADRIREP (VRS-0015). The official Croatian VTS area dataset (revised March 2025, served live 30 Sep 2026) places most of the scheme in VTS Croatia Sector A (Croatian EEZ surveillance sector); VTS Croatia is named as an ADRIREP shore-based authority in MSC.598(111) (May 2026), confirming current operation. Coordinates place the scheme outside the Trieste, Koper and Venice VTS areas.

VTS sources: SRC-2042; SRC-2043; SRC-2028; SRC-2025; SRC-2029; SRC-2032; SRC-2033. Reporting sources: SRC-2028; SRC-003.

Unresolved: None for classification. Overlap measured from dataset geometry against COLREG.2/Circ.54 lane limits; Sector A offers information service on request only (no traffic organisation service).

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: MRS only (ADRIREP) verified; Trieste VTS geometry now checked and excludes this scheme. Superseded by Pass 4 (see above).

### TSS-0107 In the Adriatic Sea: approaches to Gulf of Venice

ADRIREP applies. Venice VTS (operational, on the national list of 11 centres) does not cover this TSS: its own manual lists the IMO scheme as outside the VTS area, and coordinates show it ends about 1 nm outside the outer limit. Vessels must call Venice VTS 5 NM before the outer limit, which may fall inside the TSS, but this is a first-call point, not evidence of monitoring the scheme.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2028; SRC-003; SRC-2033.

Unresolved: None for classification. Whether Venice VTS monitors the scheme outside its area (decision 1) has no official evidence.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed: MRS only; first-pass audit claim that the Venice manual places this TSS in the VTS area corrected (that is a local scheme; the IMO scheme is in Annex 4, outside).

### TSS-0108 In the Gulf of Trieste

Koper VTS (operational from 6 January 2026) charts the eastern end of the Gulf of Trieste TSS inside its area and sets lane-use rules; the official Croatian VTS dataset (March 2025) places the southern part of the scheme in VTS Croatia Sector B; VTS Croatia is an ADRIREP shore authority in MSC.598(111). Part of the scheme (northern lane west of the precautionary area) is in neither area. ADRIREP applies.

VTS sources: SRC-2029; SRC-2030; SRC-2031; SRC-2042; SRC-2043; SRC-2028; SRC-2025; SRC-2032. Reporting sources: SRC-2028; SRC-003.

Unresolved: Combined share covered by Koper VTS and VTS Croatia not measured against the Koper chart; Slovenian and Croatian VTS areas reflect differing boundary positions (Piran Bay) and may overlap; VTS Trieste manual text-versus-polygon conflict.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: Unresolved to VTS + MRS (Koper VTS partial, from the current manual chart).

### TSS-0109 Approaches to/from Koper

Koper VTS manual (Nov 2025) charts the Koper approaches TSS inside the VTS area and sets lane-use rules; gov.si states Koper VTS manages the TSS; services began 6 January 2026. ADRIREP applies.

VTS sources: SRC-2029; SRC-2030; SRC-2031; SRC-2025. Reporting sources: SRC-2028; SRC-003.

Unresolved: none.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: Unresolved to VTS + MRS (Koper VTS confirmed; new VTS entity for coordinator to mint).

### TSS-0110 Approaches to/from Monfalcone

VTS Trieste (one of 11 Italian VTS centres on the current Guardia Costiera list; named ADRIREP shore authority in MSC.598(111), May 2026) has a VTS area drawn to include the Monfalcone TSS; coordinates and chart confirm the scheme lies inside. No separate Monfalcone VTS centre on the national list. ADRIREP applies.

VTS sources: SRC-2032; SRC-2034; SRC-2025; SRC-2028. Reporting sources: SRC-2028; SRC-003.

Unresolved: Currency flag (decision 4): boundary evidence is the 2018 manual, still the edition published on the official portal; later official sources (portal list 2026, MSC.598(111) 2026) confirm VTS Trieste operates but do not restate the boundary. The northern VTS limit passes only about 0.03' north of the TSS northern end at 013°36.5'E.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: MRS only to VTS + MRS (VTS Trieste polygon checked).

## Method notes

Accessed 30 September 2026. Geometry: TSS coordinates from the COLREG.2 circulars (Circ.54, Circ.58, Circ.51, Circ.60, A.338(IX)) compared with official VTS limit coordinates and the VTS area charts in the Koper, Trieste and Venice manuals (images rendered and inspected). Circular coordinates may have been amended before the 2025 Ships' Routeing edition; the baseline edition itself was not reopened. ADRIREP: MSC.139(76) Part I text is in force until 30 Nov 2026 and MSC.598(111) replaces it from 1 Dec 2026 with the same area (north of 40°25'N); VRS-0015 applies to both. Greek and Egyptian official sites returned 403 or connection resets (hcg.gr, hellenicparliament.gr, ynanp.gr, dpa.gov.eg); NGA MSI API rejected requests; B23 therefore remains Unresolved. Italian manuals are the editions currently published on the Guardia Costiera portal (2017/2018 text, files re-uploaded Sep 2024); the VTS Trieste link (0110) carries a decision 4 currency flag. No VTS IDs allocated; Koper VTS and VTS Trieste are new entities not in the existing register. Press items (Al-Mal 2024, Qatar News Agency July 2026) used only as leads.
