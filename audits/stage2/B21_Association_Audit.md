## Record detail

### TSS-0101 Off Cape La Nao

No mandatory reporting (Spain's official list of mandatory systems excludes Cabo de la Nao; no Part I system). VTS absence not established: official 2025 lists of SASEMAR DST supervision omit Cabo de la Nao, but no official complete list of Spanish VTS or area chart excludes it.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-003; SRC-2021; SRC-096.

Unresolved: Need an official Spanish statement or chart showing whether any VTS covers the TSS, or an official complete list of Spanish VTS areas; Valencia port VTS and Alicante/Dénia port traffic limits not overlaid (the TSS is far offshore, so coverage is unlikely but not disproved). Leads: CCS Valencia, Valencia port VTS.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: Unresolved to Neither confirmed (2025 official Spanish lists exclude Cabo de la Nao). Superseded by Pass 4 (see above).

### TSS-0102 In the Corsica Channel

TSS identity confirmed (COLREG.2/Circ.67, in force 1 Dec 2016). No Part I scheme; no VTS established by official source.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2013; SRC-003.

Unresolved: Whether CROSS Med (La Garde/Ajaccio) or an Italian Guardia Costiera VTS (e.g. Livorno) monitors the TSS as a VTS; whether any French préfet maritime or Italian national reporting obligation applies. Pass 4 lead: arrêté du préfet maritime de la Méditerranée n° 256/2016 of 24 November 2016 regulates navigation in the Corsica Channel DST, precautionary areas and inshore zone; not opened (not found on the premar-mediterranee.gouv.fr listing), may contain reporting or monitoring provisions.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: Neither confirmed to Unresolved (no authoritative evidence of absence; BONIFREP exclusion confirmed).

### TSS-0105 In the Adriatic Sea: in the North Adriatic Sea

Inside ADRIREP by area. Croatian 2025 regulation extends the VTS area to the Croatian EEZ and gives the VTS service supervision of the IMO Adriatic TSS; overlay against the Italy-Croatia shelf line puts the whole eastern part of the North Adriatic TSS on the Croatian side. The western part is mainly on the Italian side and outside the Venice VTS area; Italian VTS coverage of it not established. VTS partial.

VTS sources: SRC-2020; SRC-2025; SRC-2018; SRC-2019; SRC-2024; SRC-2017. Reporting sources: SRC-2016; SRC-2017; SRC-2018; SRC-2019; SRC-003.

Unresolved: (1) Ships' Routeing 2025 text of B-III/8 not opened; composition (eastern and western parts) inferred from COLREG.2/Circ.54 and the register numbering. (2) Croatian EEZ decision and 2005 technical correction of the shelf line not opened; Sector A limits (NN 52/2025 annex 4 image) not overlaid; datum differences (ED50/Hermannskogel) not applied, immaterial at 6 NM or more. (3) Italian coverage of the western part (Ravenna/Ancona Coast Guard) not established.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: MRS only to VTS + MRS (VTS Croatia, partial, NN 52/2025); ADRIREP VRS-0015 confirmed with both regimes recorded.

## Method notes

Accessed 2026-09-30. Budget about 6 to 10 web actions per TSS, reusing Spanish (SASEMAR/Ministry) and IMO sources across neighbouring TSS. IMO circulars COLREG.2/Circ.55, 55/Corr.1, 57 and 67 were read from copies on a Shanghai Maritime University server (IMO document text; host is third-party). salvamentomaritimo.es and mit.gov.it serve incomplete certificate chains; the missing intermediate certificates were fetched from the issuers' AIA URLs and added to a local CA bundle (verification kept on). REMPEC and IHO Tunisian reports could not be retrieved (expired certificate / proxy 502). The Ships' Routeing 2025 edition was not available, so TSS positions come from the latest IMO circulars or Croatian notices found; this is flagged per record. Overlay for TSS-0096 used Mercator interpolation of the legal limit lines. New services (Tarifa Traffic, Tangier Traffic, CCS Almería DST service, VTS Croatia) are not in the existing VTS register; no IDs allocated. Decisions 1 (Tarifa, Almería), 2 (WETREP) and 4 (Tangier caveat) applied.
