## Evidence check

### TSS-0036 — Off Ushant

IMO Part B B-II/4 directly references both **WETREP** and the mandatory reporting system **Off Ushant (OUESSREP)**.

The current CROSS Corsen 2024 activity report, published in 2026, identifies the coastal VTS mission in the Ouessant TSS and records mandatory reports handled by **Ouessant Traffic**.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0037 — Off Casquets

IMO Part B B-II/5 directly references both **WETREP** and **MANCHEREP**.

French official material designates CROSS Jobourg as the VTS for the Casquets TSS and adjacent coastal area and states that it operates the mandatory reporting system. Current 2026 Préfecture maritime material confirms CROSS Jobourg remains actively monitoring vessels in the Casquets TSS.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0038 — In the Strait of Dover and adjacent waters

Current MCA guidance is direct:
- the Dover Strait is covered by the IMO TSS;
- Channel VTS provides 24-hour radar and radio safety service;
- the service is jointly operated from Dover and CROSS Gris-Nez;
- CALDOVREP is the mandatory reporting system for the area.

IMO Part B also explicitly links the TSS to WETREP.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0039 — At West Hinder

IMO 2025 Figure B-II/7 shows both the **CALDOVREP** and **WETREP** reporting-area boundaries in relation to the West Hinder TSS. The CALDOVREP eastern boundary cuts the western end of the routeing complex; WETREP covers the Belgian offshore area. Reporting applicability remains category- and position-dependent.

The current VTS-Scheldt VHF-sector map places the western VTS boundary close to the eastern termination of the West Hinder routeing complex. However, the opened source does not explicitly state that the IMO TSS itself is inside the VTS area. Stage 2 therefore does not create a positive VTS relationship.

Classification: **MRS only**.

### TSS-0040 — Sunk South

IMO B-II/8 directly links the integrated Sunk routeing system to **WETREP**.

Current PLA 2026 material states that **Sunk VTS** covers the Sunk Inner Precautionary Area and TSS and is run by the MCA from CNIS Dover. A current 2026 MCA submission confirms MCA is the national competent authority and HM Coastguard provides day-to-day operation.

Current planning evidence also describes Sunk VTS as covering the Sunk precautionary areas and their associated TSSs. Sunk South is explicitly one of the three TSS in the integrated scheme.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

## Duplicate check

- OUESSREP and WETREP are separate mandatory schemes at Ushant.
- MANCHEREP and WETREP are separate mandatory schemes at Casquets.
- CALDOVREP and WETREP overlap for relevant traffic in/around Dover and West Hinder.
- One Channel VTS entity is retained for Dover/Gris-Nez.
- Sunk VTS is separate from London VTS and Harwich Haven VTS.
- VTS-Scheldt is not linked to West Hinder without explicit TSS coverage evidence.
