## Evidence check

### Russian eastern-Gulf TSS: TSS-0001 to TSS-0003

Current Rosmorport material states that the regional VTS in the eastern Gulf of Finland operates in Russian territorial waters and that the Saint Petersburg VTS area covers established vessel routes east of longitude 026°30'E. It identifies Sommers and Gogland as radar/technical posts and confirms H24 operation.

Current ADMIRALTY Weekly Edition 11/2026 describes Saint Petersburg Coastal VTS, its Sector 1/2 split, and repeater stations at Gogland and Sommers. It is based on the Russian Annual Notice 2026.

The IMO TSS positions are consistent with this operational area:
- Sommers lies east of 027°32'E and crosses the 028°00'E sector split.
- Hogland lies around 026°57'E to 027°32'E, in Sector 1.
- Rodsher lies immediately east of 026°30'E. Earlier Russian official navigation rules explicitly list the Rodsher TSS in the coastal VTS area; the current Rosmorport area description confirms the service remains in force over established routes east of 026°30'E.

The three are therefore associated with the current Russian VTS service.

### GOFREP: TSS-0004 and TSS-0005

GOFREP remains an IMO mandatory ship reporting system. Current Fintraffic material states that the four Gulf of Finland TSS associated with the reporting system are south of Hankoniemi, Porkkala and Kalbådagrund, plus west of Kõpu. It separately notes Russian-maintained TSS in Russian territorial waters.

MSC.231(82) places the GOFREP Central Reporting Line through the midpoints of the Porkkala and Kalbådagrund separation zones. These two associations are direct.

The same sources distinguish GOFREP from the Finnish and Estonian VTS areas. Therefore Helsinki Traffic's GOFREP role is not treated as Helsinki VTS coverage of these offshore TSS.
