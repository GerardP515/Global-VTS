## Evidence check

### TSS-0041 — Sunk East

IMO B-II/8 identifies Sunk East as one of the three TSS in the integrated Sunk routeing scheme and directly refers to mandatory WETREP.

Current MCA/PLA evidence confirms Sunk VTS remains operational. Current 2026 navigation-planning material states that Sunk VTS covers the two Sunk precautionary areas and the TSSs and other routes leading into them.

Sunk East is therefore associated with Sunk VTS and WETREP.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0042 — Sunk North

The same evidence applies independently to Sunk North. It is a named TSS within the integrated Sunk routeing scheme, falls within the Sunk VTS routeing area and is linked by IMO to WETREP.

Classification: **VTS + MRS**.

### TSS-0043 — Off Brown Ridge

The new **VTS Off Texel** became operational on 3 November 2025. Netherlands Notice 41/261(P)/25 gives its exact polygon and states that it was established for the traffic separation schemes off Texel and Vlieland.

Off Brown Ridge lies around longitude 003°17'E to 003°23'E. The VTS Off Texel polygon begins around longitude 004°22'E on its south-west side. Off Brown Ridge is therefore well outside the VTS area.

No other current Dutch offshore VTS was identified for this TSS.

IMO B-II/9 does not refer to a mandatory ship reporting system. The WETREP boundary does not include the Dutch Off Friesland system.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0044 — West Friesland

West Friesland runs north-east from the central North Sea toward the Dutch coastal routeing complex.

The VTS Off Texel polygon reaches north only to about 53°37.1'N. At the southern latitudes where West Friesland is below that line, the TSS lies west of the VTS polygon. By the point West Friesland reaches the VTS longitudes, it is already north of the VTS area's northern boundary.

The two polygons therefore do not overlap.

No applicable IMO mandatory reporting system was identified.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0045 — North Friesland

North Friesland lies around 54°00'N to 54°06'N. The northernmost VTS Off Texel boundary is about 53°37.1'N.

The TSS is therefore north of that VTS area. No separate current offshore North Friesland VTS is identified by Rijkswaterstaat.

No applicable IMO mandatory reporting system was identified.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

## Duplicate check

- TSS-0041 and TSS-0042 share the existing **VTS-0042 Sunk VTS**.
- TSS-0041 and TSS-0042 share **VRS-0005 WETREP**.
- VTS Off Texel is a real current VTS, but none of TSS-0043 to TSS-0045 falls inside its polygon.
- VTS Off Texel is therefore not added as a positive association in B09 and no new VTS ID is allocated from this batch.
- Offshore traffic monitoring, AIS and route advice are not relabelled as VTS where no VTS-area evidence exists.

## Changes made

1. Added TSS-0041 to TSS-0045 to the master association register.
2. Linked Sunk East and Sunk North to Sunk VTS and WETREP.
3. Checked all three Off Friesland TSS against the exact VTS Off Texel polygon.
4. Added current Netherlands VTS Off Texel evidence and Rijkswaterstaat operating evidence.

## Unresolved issues

None that prevent Stage 2 classification.

VTS Off Texel will need to be considered again when the authoritative sequence reaches TSS Off Texel and the Vlieland schemes.
