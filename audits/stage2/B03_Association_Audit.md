## Evidence check

### TSS-0011 — North Åland Sea

The 2025 IMO baseline correctly treats North Åland Sea as a separate TSS under parent B-I/11.

Current Fintraffic material identifies operational monitoring specifically for the **South Åland Sea TSS**, not the northern TSS. Sweden's current designated VTS list does not identify an Åland Sea VTS area.

Fintraffic states that the Åland Sea TSS area has no obligatory vessel reporting system. The Swedish Transport Agency's Baltic SRS list likewise contains SOUNDREP, BELTREP, GOFREP and GDANREP only.

Sweden Traffic may monitor TSS compliance and broadcasts safety information, but that function is not a designated VTS or mandatory reporting system.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0012 — South Åland Sea

Fintraffic directly confirms that South Åland Sea TSS is monitored by **Åland Sea Traffic**, operating from the Western Finland Vessel Traffic Centre. Monitoring is by AIS, and vessels may be contacted on VHF 16 before a COLREG violation.

Fintraffic also explicitly states that the area has **no obligatory reporting system**.

Stage 2 asks whether the TSS is associated with a VTS and/or mandatory reporting scheme. Åland Sea Traffic is described as a monitoring service rather than a designated VTS area. It is therefore preserved as operational context but excluded from both Stage 2 positive counts.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0013 — West Klintehamn

The current Swedish designated VTS list contains nine areas and no Gotland/Klintehamn VTS. Current Baltic SRS material identifies no mandatory system for this TSS.

Recommended port or coastal calls do not meet the project evidence standard for a TSS-to-VTS or TSS-to-MRS relationship.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0014 — North Hoburgs Bank

The current Swedish VTS inventory contains no Hoburgs Bank/Gotland VTS area. No current Baltic mandatory SRS applies.

This TSS remains distinct from South Hoburgs Bank.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0015 — Off Öland Island

Historical material referring to Kalmarsund VTS is not treated as evidence of current operation. The current Swedish designated VTS inventory has no Kalmarsund or Öland offshore VTS area.

The Swedish Transport Agency's current Baltic SRS list identifies no mandatory reporting system here. TSFS 2026:60, due to enter into force on 1 October 2026, was also checked and does not restore a Kalmarsund/Öland offshore VTS area.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

## Duplicate check

- Åland Sea Traffic is not assigned a VTS ID because the authority describes it as monitoring of an international-water TSS, not a designated VTS.
- Sweden Traffic is not treated as a VTS merely because it monitors TSS compliance or broadcasts MSI.
