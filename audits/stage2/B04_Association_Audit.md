## Evidence check

### TSS-0016 — In Bornholmsgat

Swedish Ufs A states that Swedish Maritime Administration monitors TSS compliance. Only Off Falsterborev and The Sound are monitored by Sound VTS; the remaining Swedish TSS are monitored by Sweden Traffic.

That is important operational monitoring, but it is not evidence of a formally declared VTS area.

The Danish Maritime Authority routeing guide lists Bornholmsgat as a Baltic TSS while identifying VTS areas separately. No formal Bornholmsgat VTS or IMO mandatory ship reporting system was established.

Classification: **Neither confirmed**.

### TSS-0017 and TSS-0018 — Gulf of Gdańsk East and West

The Maritime Office in Gdynia provides direct evidence.

It states that:
- VTS Zatoka Gdańska operates H24;
- the VTS area is bounded by the external limits of the compulsory GDANREP ship reporting system;
- TSS EAST and TSS WEST are established inside the VTS area;
- vessel traffic rules within those TSS are administered through the VTS service.

The relationship is therefore direct for both TSS.

Classification: **VTS + MRS** for each.

One VTS entity and one reporting scheme are shared:
- VTS-0015 VTS Zatoka Gdańska
- VRS-0002 GDANREP

### TSS-0019 — Słupska Bank

The Maritime Office in Gdynia states that vessel traffic supervision in the TSS Ławica Słupska area is performed by **VTS Ławica Słupska / VTS Slupska Bank**.

The service:
- controls and manages vessel traffic;
- supervises compliance with the traffic rules;
- provides information using radar, communications and AIS;
- operates H24;
- uses VHF 73 and 16.

No applicable IMO Part I mandatory ship reporting system covers Słupska Bank. Local VTS reporting requirements do not create a separate IMO MRS entity.

Classification: **VTS only**.

### TSS-0020 — Adlergrund

The current BSH VTS Guide overview chart shows **Sassnitz Traffic** as an operational sector of VTS Centre Warnemünde covering the waters north-east of Rügen. The IMO Adlergrund TSS coordinates lie inside that mapped service area.

A BSH/WSA Ostsee Notice to Mariners concerning operations inside TSS Adlergrund also directs vessels to VTS Warnemünde / Sassnitz Traffic on VHF 13. This historical operational reference is used only together with the current BSH VTS-area chart.

No applicable IMO Part I mandatory reporting system covers Adlergrund.

Classification: **VTS only**.
