## Record detail

### TSS-0096 Off Cape S. Vicente

Portuguese coastal VTS (VTS-0023, Roca Control) statutory area and COPREP area share the western limit line; overlay of the 2005 amended TSS shows the scheme inside apart from edge slivers of the outermost southbound lane (about 0.3 NM on the west; about 0.85 NM at the south-east end for the VTS area only). DGRM pages (live, accessed 30 Sep 2026) confirm the coastal VTS and Roca Control are current. WETREP names the TSS.

VTS sources: SRC-2001; SRC-2002; SRC-2003; SRC-2005; SRC-2006; SRC-2000. Reporting sources: SRC-2000; SRC-2002; SRC-2004; SRC-003.

Unresolved: Overlay used the 2005 ED50 coordinates from COLREG.2/Circ.55 (ED50 to WGS84 shift of roughly 0.1 NM not applied); the Ships' Routeing 2025 description was not opened. DL 263/2009 checked as published; later amendments to art. 6 not checked. 'Partial' reflects edge slivers only; the TSS is practically within the Roca Control service.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed (VTS + MRS, VTS-0023, VRS-0012), with coordinate overlay added, extent recorded as partial by about 0.3 NM, and WETREP (VRS-0005) added.

### TSS-0097 At Banco del Hoyo

No mandatory reporting scheme covers the TSS (outside COPREP, WETREP and GIBREP by coordinates; not among Spain's stated mandatory systems). No official source was found that names Banco del Hoyo within the Tarifa Traffic service or any other VTS.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-2007; SRC-2000; SRC-2004; SRC-2008; SRC-096; SRC-003.

Unresolved: Whether Tarifa Traffic (CCS Tarifa) monitors Banco del Hoyo as part of the 'Estrecho' DST supervision, or whether CCS Cádiz port traffic service reaches it; need a Salvamento Marítimo/ALRS service-area description. Current (2025 edition) TSS coordinates not opened; A.186(VI) 1969 description used.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): confirmed as Unresolved; reporting corrected from Unresolved to No (coordinate check against COPREP, WETREP and GIBREP plus Spanish official statement).

### TSS-0098 In the Strait of Gibraltar

GIBREP names the TSS. Tarifa Traffic is confirmed as a current VTS for the Strait DST by 2025 Spanish official sources; Tangier Traffic is named in MSC.300(87) as monitoring the TSS. One reporting scheme with two shore centres.

VTS sources: SRC-2008; SRC-2009; SRC-2010; SRC-096; SRC-2021. Reporting sources: SRC-2008; SRC-096; SRC-003.

Unresolved: Tangier Traffic's current operation rests on MSC.300(87) (2010) plus Moroccan press (not opened, lead only); under decision 4 its link needs a current Moroccan official source, but the TSS status does not depend on it because Tarifa Traffic is current.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: MRS only to VTS + MRS (Tarifa Traffic confirmed by MSC.300(87) and 2025 Spanish official sources; decision 1).

### TSS-0099 Off Cabo de Gata

2025 ministerial orders name CCS Almería as supervising traffic in the Cabo de Gata DST; the official SASEMAR site lists Cabo de Gata among the DST where it monitors and assists traffic; CCS Almería is an operating VTS centre (Almería Port Control). Reporting is voluntary only.

VTS sources: SRC-2009; SRC-2010; SRC-2022; SRC-096; SRC-2023; SRC-2011. Reporting sources: SRC-003; SRC-096; SRC-2012.

Unresolved: No official source gives a distinct service name or VHF call sign for the Cabo de Gata DST traffic service (unlike 'Tarifa Traffic' and the Finisterre STM); the only IMO text (COLREG.2/Circ.57) refers to Almería MRCC for voluntary notification.

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: Unresolved to VTS only (2025 official assignment of CCS Almería to the Cabo de Gata DST; reporting confirmed voluntary).

### TSS-0100 Off Cape Palos

No mandatory reporting (Spain's official list of mandatory systems excludes Cabo de Palos; no Part I system). VTS absence not established: official 2025 lists of SASEMAR DST supervision omit Cabo de Palos, but no official complete list of Spanish VTS or area chart excludes it.

VTS sources: none. Reporting sources: SRC-003; SRC-2021; SRC-096.

Unresolved: Need an official Spanish statement or chart showing whether any VTS (SASEMAR CCS Cartagena, or a port authority service) covers the TSS, or an official complete list of Spanish VTS areas; the Cartagena port traffic service limits (port authority ordinance in BOE) were not overlaid. Leads: CCS Cartagena (port traffic monitoring).

Researcher's view of the first-pass lead (Passes 1 to 3): corrected: Unresolved to Neither confirmed (2025 official Spanish lists of DST supervision and reporting systems exclude Cabo de Palos). Superseded by Pass 4 (see above).

## Method notes

Accessed 2026-09-30. Budget about 6 to 10 web actions per TSS, reusing Spanish (SASEMAR/Ministry) and IMO sources across neighbouring TSS. IMO circulars COLREG.2/Circ.55, 55/Corr.1, 57 and 67 were read from copies on a Shanghai Maritime University server (IMO document text; host is third-party). salvamentomaritimo.es and mit.gov.it serve incomplete certificate chains; the missing intermediate certificates were fetched from the issuers' AIA URLs and added to a local CA bundle (verification kept on). REMPEC and IHO Tunisian reports could not be retrieved (expired certificate / proxy 502). The Ships' Routeing 2025 edition was not available, so TSS positions come from the latest IMO circulars or Croatian notices found; this is flagged per record. Overlay for TSS-0096 used Mercator interpolation of the legal limit lines. New services (Tarifa Traffic, Tangier Traffic, CCS Almería DST service, VTS Croatia) are not in the existing VTS register; no IDs allocated. Decisions 1 (Tarifa, Almería), 2 (WETREP) and 4 (Tangier caveat) applied.
