## Duplicate control

Ras Tanura Terminal and Ju’aymah Crude SPM are **one Aramco port group** and one VTMS. One service for TSS-0136.

B-IV/15 is one IMO parent with two named north schemes (0139, 0140). Count two TSS, one Kuwait oil-port control family.

Marjan/Zuluf MTCC is not Ras Tanura VTMS.

---

## Evidence

Aramco *Ports and Terminals*, July 2025:

- Mandatory VTMS in the Port of Ras Tanura.
- Information service only; operators do not assume conduct of the vessel.
- “The separation zones and routes shown on the charts of the Ras Tanura Channel are approved by IMO.”
- Report to Ras Tanura Port Control VHF 10 at the entry buoy; shift to 13 at “A” buoy.

Khafji: COLREG.2/Circ.54 annex describes the approach TSS. NGA lists the oil terminal on VHF 16, not a VTS call sign.

Kuwait: NGA Pub. 172 records an IMO TSS off Mina al Ahmadi and permission from the Mina al Ahmadi control tower on VHF 69 to enter the security zone. That is port/security control. A dedicated VTS users’ manual was not opened.
