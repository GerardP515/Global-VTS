## Identity traps

**Corsica Channel ≠ Strait of Bonifacio.**

- TSS-0102 is the channel east of Corsica toward Capraia / Elba.
- VRS-0014 BONIFREP / Bonifacio Traffic is the strait between Corsica and Sardinia, ~20 M radius on Bonifacio. Italian IIM 20/2025 still describes that system.
- Attaching BONIFREP to TSS-0102 would be a classification error.

Cape La Nao is Cabo de la Nao (Xàbia), not Cape Palos (B20 TSS-0100).

## VRS-0015 ADRIREP — TSS-0105

Area: whole Adriatic Sea north of 40°25'.00 N (MSC.139(76); restated in MSC.598(111) annex 2.1).

The North Adriatic TSS sits inside that sea. Association: **confirmed by area**.

**Current participation (MSC.139(76), still in force until 1 Dec 2026):** oil tankers ≥150 GT; ships ≥300 GT carrying dangerous or polluting goods.

**From 0000 UTC 1 December 2026 (MSC.598(111)):**

- all oil tankers ≥150 GT;
- all ships ≥10,000 GT;
- all other ships carrying hazardous cargo in bulk or in package.

Sectors and first-report authority remain a multi-state service (Brindisi and others). One VRS. Later B22 Trieste / Venice / Koper records will use the same VRS-0015, not a new ID.

UKHO Wk 20/26 is already rewriting ALRS ADRIREP pages — use the IMO annex as primary when the two differ.
