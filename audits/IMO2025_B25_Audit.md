## Duplicate control — Turkish Straits

Five IMO TSS (TSS-0120 in B24 plus 0121–0124 here) sit inside **one** service family:

- TSVTS = Istanbul VTS + Çanakkale VTS
- TUBRAP = national reporting for the Marmara Region (Istanbul Strait, Sea of Marmara, Çanakkale Strait)

Sectors are working channels, not extra VTS and not extra reporting schemes.

| TSS | Working sector (from TSVTS guide) |
|---|---|
| TSS-0120 North approach | Türkeli Ch 11 |
| TSS-0121 Strait of Istanbul | Kandilli Ch 12, Kadıköy Ch 13 |
| TSS-0122 South approach / Marmara | Marmara Ch 14 |
| TSS-0123 Strait of Çanakkale | Gelibolu Ch 11, Nara Ch 12, Kumkale Ch 13 |
| TSS-0124 Çanakkale SW approach | Kumkale Ch 13; VTS south limit near 39°44'N / 025°47'E |

Publication estimate: one TSVTS / TUBRAP entry with directional diagrams for north and south passages, not five VTS chapters.

## Gulf of Suez vs Suez Canal

| System | Area | Source |
|---|---|---|
| IMO TSS “In the Gulf of Suez” | Gulf lanes (paper: from ~5 M S of Ras El Adabiya to E of Shaker Island) | B-IV/1 |
| GOS VTIMS / GOS VTMS | Red Sea and Gulf of Suez surveillance; NGA: compulsory >300 t | NGA Pub. 172 §2.1; EAMS / academic GOS VTMS |
| SCA SC-VTMS | Canal and Port Said / Port Tewfik approaches (~19 M radar) | SCA Rules of Navigation; B23–B24 Port Said TSS |

Do not collapse GOS VTIMS into SC-VTMS. Canal reporting remains SCA procedure, not Part I.

NGA compulsory-participation wording is a national VTS rule. It is not evidence of an IMO Part I SRS.
