## Planned worldwide source hierarchy

The project should systematically check:

1. IMO *Ships’ Routeing*.
2. Current IMO routeing and reporting amendments.
3. UKHO Annual Summary of Notices to Mariners.
4. ADMIRALTY List of Radio Signals Volume 6.
5. ITU List IV / MARS.
6. National maritime administrations.
7. National coastguards.
8. Operating VTS authorities.
9. Official hydrographic publications.
10. Official port and waterway authorities.

## Source rule

Third-party material may identify a research lead.

It must not, by itself, establish current operational VTS status or a final worldwide count.
