> **Important:** The 13 unresolved TSS are **not confirmed as “No VTS”**. They remain undetermined because the available evidence does not yet support either a positive service association or a defensible negative finding.

This is the final **TSS-derived** service register. It is not a worldwide census of every VTS.

## Counting rule

One row represents one canonical operational service identity. Sectors, aliases, multiple radio channels and repeated TSS links do not create additional service identities.

The project retains two count classes:

- **Core** — VTS / VTIS / VTMS / qualifying traffic-control services counted in the core service total.
- **Supplemental** — explicit TSS monitoring/information services that qualify under the project rules but are not asserted as formal VTS for the relevant association.
