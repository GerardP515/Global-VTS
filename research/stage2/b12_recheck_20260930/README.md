This is a scoped recheck of TSS-0056 to TSS-0060 on 30 September 2026.

- `master_patch.json` contains field updates with a SHA-256 guard for each original TSS row.
- `source_patch.json` refreshes four existing source definitions. No identifiers are allocated.
- `before_records.json` preserves the five earlier records.
- `coordinate_extraction.json` contains coordinate facts transcribed from the supplied IMO PDF and the official VTS notice.
- `check_geometry.py` reproduces overlap and sensitivity checks using Shapely and pyproj.
- `geometry_checks.json` is the recorded output, not a certified chart or navigational boundary.
- `retrieval_manifest.json` records successful downloads, checksums and failed attempts separately.
- `B12_Association_Rows.csv` is the updated five-record extract, generated by the scoped updater.
- `application_checks.json` records the unchanged 219 other master rows and protected B20–B29 paths.

The optional ED50 interpretation and local endpoint tie are sensitivity checks only.
The 200 m test is not a legal boundary buffer or navigation tolerance.
No full IMO publication or authority chart is republished here.
