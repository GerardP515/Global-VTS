## Instrument change dates

### VRS-0015 ADRIREP — In the Adriatic Sea

| Date | Event | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| 5 December 2002 | Adopted | MSC.139(76) |
| 1 July 2003 | Entered into force | MSC.139(76) |
| 22 May 2026 | Revised system adopted | MSC.598(111) |
| **1 December 2026 0000 UTC** | **Revised ADRIREP implemented** | MSC.598(111) |

Until 1 December 2026 use MSC.139(76) participation: oil tankers ≥150 GT; ships ≥300 GT carrying dangerous or polluting goods.

From 1 December 2026 use MSC.598(111): oil tankers ≥150 GT; all ships ≥10,000 GT; all other ships carrying hazardous cargo in bulk or in package.

Affects TSS-0105 onward through the Adriatic batches (B21–B22 at least). One VRS ID. Do not mint a second ADRIREP.

UKHO ALRS rewrites (e.g. Wk 20/26) may lag the IMO annex. Prefer the IMO text when they differ.
