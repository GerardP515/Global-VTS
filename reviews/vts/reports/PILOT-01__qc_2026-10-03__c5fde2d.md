## 3. Findings requiring disposition

### QA-01 | ERROR | Canonical south-westbound timing loses part of the recorded instruction

- Affected: `directions[P01-SW].reporting_events[SW-ENTRY]`, `EV-SW-TIMING`, conflict C04 and narrative section 2.
- Draft: YAML retains only the MGN timing limit. The narrative also includes the North Foreland VHF-range condition.
- Evidence: SRC-004, Mandatory reporting, SW paragraph; SRC-005, section 3.10; P01-IMO85, Annex 2 section 3.3, PDF page 11.
- Assessment: The MGN wording is not falsely transcribed. The defect is incomplete reconciliation in the canonical data.
- Required action: Preserve the published conditions and their source-specific wording in the canonical event. Link EV-SW-TIMING to that event.
- Export control: The human summary and a YAML-generated spread must not produce different timing instructions.
- Do not import the NE two-mile condition into the SW event.

### QA-02 | ERROR | Field W no longer explicitly requests a count

- Affected: `report_types[RT-CALDOVREP].fields[FLD-12].information_required` and EV-F12.
- Draft: `Persons carried aboard.`
- Evidence: P01-IMO251, Appendix W, PDF page 3 / printed Annex 29 page 2.
- Assessment: The source asks for a count, not merely a description of the persons carried.
- Required action: State that the total number aboard must be supplied. Preserve the source locator and applicability.

### QA-03 | OMISSION | Field Q/R is compressed beyond a safe reporting prompt

- Affected: `report_types[RT-CALDOVREP].fields[FLD-10]` and EV-F10.
- Draft: A short reference to faults and circumstances affecting normal navigation.
- Evidence: P01-IMO251, Appendix Q or R, PDF page 3.
- Assessment: The shortened prompt does not retain the explicit structural, cargo and equipment dimensions of the reporting subject.
- Required action: Restore the full categories in the working reporting prompt, without narrowing them to steering or propulsion faults.
- This finding concerns completeness of the paraphrase, not an incorrect report code.

### QA-04 | OMISSION | Mixed conditionality within X needs a field-part model

- Affected: `report_types[RT-CALDOVREP].fields[FLD-13]`, EV-F13 and the draft schema.
- Draft: Two subjects are combined in one field with one free-text applicability condition.
- Evidence: P01-IMO251, Appendix X, PDF page 3.
- Assessment: The human wording distinguishes the bunker threshold correctly. Automatic field selection remains ambiguous.
- Required action: Represent the two subjects as separately identified subitems, or identify exactly which subitem the condition governs.
- Test: A vessel below the bunker threshold must not lose the other subject through a whole-field filter.
- Preserve one official X code group. Do not increase the official group count merely by modelling its subitems.

### QA-05 | OMISSION | Conditional reporting for English ITZ use needs an explicit scope disposition

- Affected: directional exceptions, event population and missing scope decisions.
- Evidence: Held SRC-005 section 3.4; SRC-004, Inshore traffic zones.
- Assessment: The held guidance contains a conditional notification relevant to an altered transit. It is not represented in the event model.
- Required action: Add an evidenced conditional event, or explicitly exclude that movement with a recorded editorial scope decision.
- Do not convert notification into permission to breach Rule 10.
- Related scope check: Review SRC-005 sections 3.9 and 5.1(iv) for smaller-vessel conditions and tow-specific reporting advice.
- Those additional cases must retain their actual applicability and strength. Do not make every special case universal or mandatory.
- Harbour-entry, ferry and recreational/diving variants need not be fully developed in this bounded sample, but exclusions must be explicit.

### QA-06 | OMISSION | The source already contains a qualified alternative for confidential cargo information

- Affected: communications alternatives, report delivery conditions and G04.
- Evidence: P01-IMO85, Annex 2, opening paragraph on PDF page 11 and section 5 on PDF page 13.
- Assessment: The base instrument describes advance non-verbal delivery for the commercially sensitive cargo portion.
- Required action: Record this documented option and its limited scope, then check current implementation and delivery details.
- Retain G04 for unresolved current methods, listening watch, failure procedures and AIS field acceptance.

- Source retention and current-notice reconciliation are still prerequisites for publication.
